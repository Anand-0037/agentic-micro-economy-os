# Public-good statement (BGA)

Autonomous treasuries already move capital programmatically — DAOs, foundations, and microfinance pools. Without verifiable cognition, the public cannot independently audit *why* capital moved. That gap enables hidden policy bypass, unaccountable agent behavior, and loss of trust in autonomous finance.

AMEO treats **auditability as public infrastructure**. The Narrative Console reconstructs every cognition cycle from append-only worker events: observation, planner output, hard policy guardrails, Mantle Sepolia settlement, ERC-8004 identity proofs, and 0G Storage receipts. Nothing is mocked; empty states are honest.

## Open infrastructure

- MIT-licensed codebase — fork, extend, and deploy your own agent identity.
- Public 0G reasoning traces when configured — anyone can verify rationale anchors.
- Formal policy predicates (`docs/policy-spec.mdx`) evaluated outside the LLM — models cannot bypass guardrails.

## Public-interest applications

1. **DAO grant treasuries** — programmatic disbursements with on-chain `DecisionLogged` proofs for every material action.
2. **Foundation endowments** — policy-bound rebalancing with independent replay for auditors and boards.
3. **Microfinance pools** — transparent allocation cycles with Mantle settlement and verifiable cognition for regulators and communities.

## Anti-misuse stance

When every remote LLM provider fails, AMEO falls back to `local_rules` — a deterministic safety planner that still enforces policy and can refuse to act. Safety is not optional; it is the default when inference is unavailable. Unsafe plans are rejected and surfaced in replay, not silently overridden.

AMEO is built so **any** autonomous treasury can be independently audited — not just ours.
